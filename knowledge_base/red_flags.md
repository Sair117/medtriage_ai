# Red Flag Symptoms — Immediate Escalation Required

These symptoms require immediate escalation to a human clinician regardless of other context.

## Cardiovascular Red Flags
- Chest pain or pressure, especially with radiation to left arm, jaw, or back
- Sudden onset severe shortness of breath
- Chest pain with sweating, nausea, or feeling of impending doom
- Sudden severe leg swelling with pain (possible deep vein thrombosis)
- Coughing up blood

## Neurological Red Flags
- Sudden facial droop, arm weakness, or speech slurring (stroke signs — FAST)
- Sudden worst headache of life ("thunderclap headache")
- Sudden vision loss or double vision
- New onset seizure
- Sudden confusion or inability to speak
- Loss of consciousness
- Weakness or numbness on one side of the body

## Respiratory Red Flags
- Inability to speak in full sentences due to breathlessness
- Blue or grey discoloration of lips or fingertips (cyanosis)
- Choking or airway obstruction
- Severe asthma attack not responding to inhaler

## Trauma Red Flags
- Uncontrolled bleeding
- Suspected spinal injury (do not move)
- Penetrating wound to chest, abdomen, or head
- Burns covering large body area or involving face/airway
- Severe head injury with altered consciousness

## Allergic / Anaphylaxis Red Flags
- Swelling of tongue, lips, or throat
- Difficulty breathing after exposure to allergen
- Widespread hives with breathing difficulty
- Known severe allergy with accidental exposure

## Mental Health Red Flags
- Active suicidal ideation with a plan or means
- Self-harm in progress
- Acute psychosis with risk to self or others
- Severe agitation or violence

## Pediatric Red Flags (if patient mentions a child)
- Infant under 3 months with fever >38°C / 100.4°F
- Child with rash that does not blanch (non-blanching / petechial rash)
- Severe dehydration in a child (no wet diapers, sunken eyes, lethargic)
- Difficulty breathing with rib retraction in a child

## Pregnancy Red Flags
- Severe abdominal pain during pregnancy
- Heavy vaginal bleeding during pregnancy
- Sudden severe headache or vision changes in pregnancy (pre-eclampsia signs)
- Reduced or absent fetal movement after 28 weeks
