# Department Routing Protocols

## Emergency Medicine
- Receives all Level 1 (Emergency) triage cases
- Chest pain with cardiac features → cardiology consult after stabilization
- Trauma cases with suspected fractures → orthopedics consult after stabilization
- Any patient with altered consciousness or hemodynamic instability

## Cardiology
- Chest pain or tightness without emergency features
- Palpitations or irregular heartbeat
- Known heart condition follow-up
- High blood pressure concerns
- Swollen ankles with history of heart problems
- Pre-operative cardiac clearance

## Orthopedics
- Bone, joint, or muscle injuries
- Back pain (non-emergency)
- Sports injuries
- Suspected fractures (stable, non-emergency)
- Chronic joint conditions (arthritis, tendinitis)
- Post-surgical orthopedic follow-up

## Dermatology
- Skin rashes lasting more than a few days
- Mole changes (size, shape, color)
- Chronic skin conditions (eczema, psoriasis)
- Acne
- Hair or nail disorders
- Skin infections not requiring emergency care

## Gastroenterology
- Persistent abdominal pain (non-emergency)
- Chronic digestive issues (reflux, IBS)
- Changes in bowel habits
- Difficulty swallowing
- Unexplained weight loss with GI symptoms
- Liver concerns (jaundice without emergency features)

## Neurology
- Recurring headaches or migraines
- Numbness or tingling (chronic)
- Tremors or movement disorders
- Memory problems or cognitive changes
- Dizziness or vertigo (non-emergency)
- Epilepsy follow-up

## Respiratory / Pulmonology
- Chronic cough (>3 weeks)
- Asthma management
- Shortness of breath on exertion (non-emergency)
- COPD follow-up
- Recurrent respiratory infections
- Sleep apnea concerns

## ENT (Ear, Nose & Throat)
- Ear pain or hearing loss
- Chronic sore throat or voice changes
- Sinus problems
- Nosebleeds (recurrent, non-emergency)
- Tonsil issues
- Balance problems of inner ear origin

## Mental Health
- Anxiety or depression (non-crisis)
- Insomnia
- Stress management
- Grief counseling
- Panic disorder
- Medication management for psychiatric conditions
- NOTE: Active suicidal ideation → Emergency, not Mental Health

## General Practice
- General check-ups and health screenings
- Fever or flu-like symptoms
- Fatigue or general malaise
- Vaccine inquiries
- Conditions that don't clearly fit other departments
- Multi-system complaints requiring initial assessment
